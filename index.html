Our Story

Born from a
family table

Pearl&Thyme started in 2018 with a simple belief: that India's food is extraordinary, and deserves to be treated that way. No shortcuts. No compromises.

Every recipe has a lineage. The dal makhani is from a grandmother's handwritten notebook. The butter chicken took three years of quiet refinement. Nothing on the menu exists without a reason.

Behind the Stove

The hands behind
the flavour

Chef Rajesh spent 18 years across Mumbai, Delhi and London before returning with one mission: prove that modern Indian food needs no apology.

His philosophy is ruthless simplicity — never more than seven spices per dish. Every ingredient must earn its place. The result tastes familiar, yet completely new.
